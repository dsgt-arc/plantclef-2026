## Key Findings

### 1. Sorted-prob is the 2025 method (SOLVED)
- The 2025 reference CSV was generated by **sorted-prob**: pool all per-tile
  softmax predictions, sort by probability descending, take top-9, deduplicate
- Verified as 99.9% exact match (2103/2105 images exact, 2104/2105 set match)
- This was NOT the `SubmissionTask` or `union_agg_topk` in the 2025 codebase —
  those were never used. The actual method was done outside committed code.

### 2. Probability magnitude >> tile frequency
- **Sorted-prob** (rank by probability): F1 = 0.30810, avg 5.0 species/image
- **Union** (rank by tile frequency): F1 = 0.19–0.21, uniform species counts
- A species predicted with 0.55 in one tile is more informative than a species
  predicted with 0.02 in six tiles. Sorted-prob captures this; union discards it.

### 3. Model predictions are consistent with 2025
- Per-image species overlap between 2025 and 2026 outputs is 85-92%
- The model is producing similar predictions; the difference is in post-processing

### 4. Quadrat-level aggregation hurts (unexpectedly)
- Grouping images by base quadrat (2105 images → 425 quadrats) and merging species made scores WORSE
- Our per-image species lists are too noisy for aggregation to help at this stage
